Activity 2. Muscular System

Explore & Learn

The inner body

In the first task of this section you will visit innerbody.com to explore a model of the human body and learn more about each component of its different systems. Check the muscular system model and explore the muscles that conform the arm's and hand's muscular system.

Keywords of the section: 

Muscular system, teres minor, teres major, biceps brachii, brachialis, deltoid, infraspinatus, supraspinatus, movement, strength, exercise, health.

How the muscular system works?

Watch the TED video about the muscular system and write down the 3 types of muscles and their characteristics, the composition of the muscles and how they work.
